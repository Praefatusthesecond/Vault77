> _A second brain needs hygiene._

## Daily (Optional, <2 min)

- Capture to Inbox
    
- Log in Daily Note
    

---

## Weekly Review (10-15 min)

- Empty Inbox
    
- Update active Projects
    
- Fix broken links
    
- Archive finished work
    

---

## Monthly Review

- Review Areas
    
- Promote good Inbox notes to Knowledge
    
- Delete junk without mercy
    

---

## Quarterly Check

- Review plugin list
    
- Re-read Obsidian Knowledge System
    
- Adjust rules if reality disagrees
    

---

## Decay Rules

- Notes that aren’t useful may be deleted
    
- Archiving is allowed
    
- Hoarding is not knowledge management
    

---

## Guiding Principle

> A small, sharp vault beats a large, stale one.
