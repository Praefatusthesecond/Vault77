> _Consistency reduces thinking overhead._

## Knowledge Note

**Purpose:** Capture and evolve ideas.

`type: knowledge status: evergreen`

**Rules**

- One core idea
    
- Links to related concepts
    
- No tasks unless inherent to the concept
    

---

## Project Note

**Purpose:** Track work with an outcome.

`type: project status: active`

**Must include**

- Clear goal
    
- Tasks
    
- Links to relevant knowledge
    

---

## Area Note

**Purpose:** Represent ongoing responsibilities.

`type: area`

Examples:

- Health
    
- Career
    
- Personal Development
    

---

## Inbox Note

**Purpose:** Temporary holding space.

`type: inbox`

Rules:

- No structure required
    
- Must be processed or deleted
    
- Not meant to live long-term
    

---

## Daily Note

**Purpose:** Time-based log.

`type: daily`

Rules:

- Logs, not essays
    
- Links encouraged
    
- Tasks allowed
    

---

## General Rules

- Do not invent new note types casually
    
- If you do invent one, document it here
    
- Metadata exists to support queries, not aesthetics
