> _Folders are scaffolding, not meaning._

## Canonical Folder Layout

```text
00 Meta/        → Vault rules, systems, templates
01 Inbox/       → Fast capture, zero friction
02 Knowledge/   → Atomic, evergreen ideas
03 Projects/    → Time-bound work with outcomes
04 Areas/       → Ongoing responsibilities
05 Resources/   → External references
99 Archive/     → Inactive but kept notes
```

---

## Folder Rules

### 00 Meta

- Rules about the vault itself
    
- Templates, workflows, system notes
    
- Never archived
    

---

### 01 Inbox

- Temporary
    
- Unstructured
    
- Must be reviewed regularly
    

Inbox is **not failure** — it’s a buffer.

---

### 02 Knowledge

- One idea per note
    
- No deadlines
    
- Heavy linking
    
- Long-term value
    

---

### 03 Projects

- Has a goal
    
- Has tasks
    
- Has an end
    

When done → Archive.

---

### 04 Areas

- No end date
    
- Ongoing responsibility
    
- Light task tracking
    

---

### 05 Resources

- External material
    
- Read-only mindset
    
- Not original thinking
    

---

### 99 Archive

- Closed projects
    
- Outdated notes
    
- Still searchable
    

---

## Structural Rules

- No deep nesting unless unavoidable
    
- Do not encode meaning in folder depth
    
- If unsure where something goes → Inbox
