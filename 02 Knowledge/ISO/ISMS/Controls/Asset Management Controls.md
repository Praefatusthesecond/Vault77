# Asset Management

## Purpose

The purpose of this control is to ensure that information and other associated assets are identified, inventoried, owned, and appropriately protected throughout their lifecycle.

Effective asset management ensures that assets receive a level of protection commensurate with their value, sensitivity, and criticality to the organization.

---

## Scope

This control applies to:

- All information assets, regardless of format or medium
    
- All physical and logical assets supporting information processing
    
- Assets owned, leased, or managed by the organization
    
- Assets provided by or shared with third parties

## Policy Statement

Information and other associated assets shall be identified, documented, assigned an owner, and protected throughout their lifecycle in accordance with business, legal, regulatory, and security requirements.

Assets shall be used, handled, stored, transferred, and disposed of in a controlled and secure manner.

---

## Asset Identification & Inventory

- An inventory of information and associated assets shall be maintained
    
- Assets shall be uniquely identifiable where appropriate
    
- The inventory shall include, at minimum:
    
    - Asset name or identifier
        
    - Asset type (information, software, hardware, service, etc.)
        
    - Owner
        
    - Location or system
        
    - Classification
        

The asset inventory shall be reviewed and kept up to date.

---

## Asset Ownership

- Each asset shall have a designated owner
    
- Asset owners are responsible for:
    
    - Ensuring appropriate classification
        
    - Defining protection requirements
        
    - Authorizing access
        
    - Ensuring compliance with policies
        

Ownership shall be updated when responsibilities change.

---

## Acceptable Use of Assets

- Assets shall be used only for authorized business purposes
    
- Users shall comply with documented acceptable use requirements
    
- Misuse, unauthorized use, or negligent handling of assets is prohibited
    

(See related **Acceptable Use** control.)

---

## Information Classification

- Information shall be classified based on:
    
    - Confidentiality
        
    - Integrity
        
    - Availability
        
- Classification levels shall be defined and documented
    
- Classification shall determine handling, storage, transmission, and protection requirements
    

---

## Labelling & Handling

- Information assets shall be labelled where appropriate to reflect their classification
    
- Labelling may be physical, logical, or metadata- based
    
- Handling procedures shall align with classification requirements
    

---

## Asset Lifecycle Management

Assets shall be managed throughout their lifecycle, including:

- Acquisition
    
- Use and maintenance
    
- Transfer or sharing
    
- Archiving
    
- Secure disposal or destruction
    

Controls shall ensure assets are protected at each stage.

---

## Return of Assets

- All assets issued to users or third parties shall be returned upon:
    
    - Termination of employment
        
    - End of contract
        
    - Role change, where applicable
        

Returned assets shall be verified and processed according to defined procedures.

---

## Responsibilities

### Asset Owners

- Maintain accurate asset information
    
- Ensure assets are appropriately protected
    
- Approve access and usage
    

### Users

- Use assets responsibly and according to policy
    
- Protect assets against loss, damage, or unauthorized access
    
- Report loss, theft, or misuse immediately
    

### Management

- Support asset management processes
    
- Ensure responsibilities are clearly assigned
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Non- compliance with asset management requirements may result in:

- Access restriction or removal
    
- Disciplinary action
    
- Contractual or legal consequences
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

This control shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Asset inventories remain accurate
    
- Classification remains appropriate
    
- Alignment with business, legal, and regulatory requirements
