# Third- Party Supplier Security Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that information security risks associated with third parties and suppliers are identified, assessed, and managed throughout the supplier relationship lifecycle.

Effective supplier security controls reduce the risk of information compromise, service disruption, and non- compliance arising from external parties.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All third parties, suppliers, and service providers
    
- Vendors providing access to information, systems, or facilities
    
- Outsourced services, cloud services, and managed service providers
    
- Subcontractors and supply chain partners

## Policy Statement

Information security requirements shall be defined, implemented, and monitored for all third- party relationships that may impact the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information or systems.

Supplier security controls shall be proportionate to the risk, criticality, and nature of the services provided.

---

## Supplier Security Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Supplier- related security risks are identified and assessed
    
- Security requirements are defined prior to engagement
    
- Responsibilities for information security are clearly agreed
    
- Supplier performance and compliance are monitored
    
- Security considerations apply throughout the supplier lifecycle
    

---

## Supplier Risk Assessment

- Information security risks associated with suppliers shall be assessed prior to engagement
    
- Risk assessments shall consider:
    
    - Type of access to information or systems
        
    - Data sensitivity and classification
        
    - Criticality of the service
        
    - Supplier security maturity
        
- Supplier risk shall be reviewed periodically
    

---

## Security Requirements for Suppliers

- Information security requirements shall be defined for suppliers
    
- Requirements may include:
    
    - Access control and authentication
        
    - Data protection and encryption
        
    - Incident notification obligations
        
    - Logging and monitoring
        
    - Business continuity and recovery
        
- Requirements shall be proportionate to supplier risk
    

---

## Supplier Agreements & Contracts

- Supplier agreements shall include information security clauses where appropriate
    
- Contracts shall address:
    
    - Confidentiality and data protection
        
    - Access restrictions
        
    - Subcontracting conditions
        
    - Incident reporting timelines
        
    - Right to audit or assurance mechanisms
        
- Security responsibilities shall be clearly documented
    

---

## ICT Supply Chain Security

- ICT supply chain risks shall be identified and managed
    
- Consideration shall be given to:
    
    - Dependency on critical suppliers
        
    - Concentration risk
        
    - Software and hardware provenance
        
- Changes in the supply chain shall be assessed for security impact
    

---

## Cloud Service Security

- Use of cloud services shall be subject to defined security requirements
    
- Responsibilities under shared responsibility models shall be clearly understood
    
- Cloud providers shall be assessed for security capabilities and compliance
    

---

## Supplier Access Management

- Supplier access to systems or information shall be:
    
    - Authorized
        
    - Limited to defined purposes
        
    - Time- bound where appropriate
        
- Access rights shall be reviewed and revoked when no longer required
    

---

## Monitoring & Review of Suppliers

- Supplier security performance shall be monitored periodically
    
- Monitoring may include:
    
    - Reviews or audits
        
    - Security questionnaires
        
    - Certifications or assurance reports
        
- Changes to supplier services shall be assessed for security impact
    

---

## Supplier Incident Management

- Suppliers shall be required to report security incidents without undue delay
    
- Supplier incidents affecting the organization shall be handled through incident management processes
    
- Lessons learned shall be used to improve supplier controls
    

---

## Termination & Exit Management

- Security requirements shall apply during supplier offboarding
    
- Upon termination:
    
    - Access shall be revoked
        
    - Information shall be returned or securely deleted
        
- Exit procedures shall be documented
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Supplier Owners / Contract Owners

- Assess supplier risks
    
- Ensure security requirements are defined and enforced
    

### Procurement / Legal

- Support inclusion of security requirements in contracts
    

### IT / Operations

- Implement and manage technical access controls
    

### Information Security Function

- Define supplier security standards
    
- Support risk assessments and monitoring
    
- Review supplier- related incidents and improvements
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to manage supplier security risks may result in:

- Information disclosure or loss
    
- Service disruption
    
- Regulatory or contractual consequences
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued effectiveness of supplier security measures
    
- Alignment with changing supplier relationships
    
- Compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements
