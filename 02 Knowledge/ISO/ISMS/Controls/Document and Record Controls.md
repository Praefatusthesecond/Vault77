# Document and Record Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that documents and records required by the Information Security Management System (ISMS) are properly created, maintained, controlled, protected, and retained.

Effective document and record controls ensure the availability, integrity, traceability, and reliability of information supporting the ISMS.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All ISMS- related documents and records
    
- Policies, procedures, standards, guidelines, and templates
    
- Operational records, logs, evidence, and reports
    
- Documents and records in electronic or physical form
    

---

## Control References

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Clause 7.5.1** - General
    
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Clause 7.5.2** - Creating and updating
    
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Clause 7.5.3** - Control of documented information
    

(Standard: **ISO/IEC 27001**)

---

## Policy Statement

Documented information required by the ISMS shall be controlled to ensure that it is available, accurate, up to date, protected, and used appropriately.

Records shall be maintained as evidence of conformity to ISMS requirements and retained in accordance with defined retention requirements.

---

## Types of Documented Information

### Documents

Examples include:

- Policies and standards
    
- Procedures and work instructions
    
- Guidelines and templates
    
- Plans and frameworks
    

### Records

Examples include:

- Audit results
    
- Risk assessments
    
- Training records
    
- Incident reports
    
- Change records
    
- Management review outputs
    

---

## Document Creation & Updating

- Documents shall be:
    
    - Identified with a title and unique reference where appropriate
        
    - Version controlled
        
    - Approved prior to use
        
- Changes to documents shall be reviewed and approved
    
- Obsolete documents shall be clearly identified and removed from active use
    

---

## Document Control

The organization shall ensure that:

- Current versions of documents are available where needed
    
- Documents are protected against unauthorized modification or deletion
    
- Access to documents is restricted based on need- to- know
    
- Distribution of documents is controlled
    

---

## Record Control

Records shall be:

- Protected against loss, damage, or unauthorized alteration
    
- Stored in secure and approved locations
    
- Legible, retrievable, and traceable
    
- Retained in accordance with defined retention periods
    

Records shall not be modified once finalized, unless correction procedures are defined.

---

## Access & Protection

- Access to documents and records shall be restricted based on role and responsibility
    
- Sensitive documents and records shall be protected using appropriate technical and organizational controls
    
- Backup and recovery measures shall apply to critical documented information
    

---

## Retention & Disposal

- Retention periods shall be defined for documents and records
    
- Documents and records shall not be retained longer than necessary
    
- Secure disposal methods shall be used when retention periods expire
    

---

## External Documents

- External documents relevant to the ISMS shall be identified and controlled
    
- Changes to external documents shall be monitored where applicable
    
- Use of outdated external documents shall be prevented
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Document Owners

- Ensure documents are accurate, approved, and up to date
    
- Review documents periodically
    

### Users

- Use only approved and current documents
    
- Protect documents and records under their control
    

### Information Security Function

- Define document and record control requirements
    
- Monitor compliance
    
- Support audits and continual improvement
    

---

## Monitoring & Compliance

- Compliance with document and record controls shall be monitored
    
- Deviations shall be addressed through corrective actions
    
- Records shall support audit and certification activities
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued effectiveness
    
- Alignment with ISMS and business needs
    
- Compliance with ISO/IEC 27001 requirements
