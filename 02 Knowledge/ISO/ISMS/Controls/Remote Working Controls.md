# Remote Working Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that information security risks associated with remote working are identified and managed, and that information and information systems remain protected when accessed outside organizational premises.

Effective remote working controls help maintain confidentiality, integrity, and availability regardless of work location.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All employees, contractors, and third parties working remotely
    
- All remote work locations, including home offices and temporary locations
    
- All devices and systems used to access organizational information remotely
    
- All forms of remote access, including VPN, cloud services, and remote desktops
    

---

## Control Reference

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Annex A 6.7** - Remote working
    

(Standard: **ISO/IEC 27001**)

---

## Policy Statement

Remote working shall be permitted only when appropriate security controls are in place to protect information and systems from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss.

Remote working arrangements shall be risk- based and aligned with organizational security requirements.

---

## Remote Working Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Remote access is authorized and controlled
    
- Information is protected regardless of location
    
- Devices used for remote work are secured
    
- Remote working risks are understood by personnel
    

---

## Authorization of Remote Working

- Remote working arrangements shall be formally authorized
    
- Authorization may be role- based or individual
    
- Access rights shall reflect remote working requirements and risks
    

---

## Remote Access Security

- Secure remote access solutions shall be used
    
- Remote connections shall be authenticated and authorized
    
- Multi- factor authentication shall be used where required
    
- Remote access shall be logged and monitored
    

---

## Device Security

- Devices used for remote work shall be:
    
    - Approved by the organization
        
    - Configured according to security standards
        
    - Protected by appropriate security controls
        
- Lost or stolen devices shall be reported immediately
    

---

## Network & Environment Security

- Remote workers shall use secure network connections
    
- Public or shared networks shall be treated as untrusted
    
- Additional protections shall be applied when using public networks
    

---

## Information Protection During Remote Work

- Information shall be handled in accordance with classification and handling requirements
    
- Sensitive information shall not be exposed to unauthorized persons
    
- Clear desk and clear screen principles shall apply to remote locations
    

---

## Use of Personal Devices (BYOD)

- Use of personal devices for remote work shall be:
    
    - Explicitly approved
        
    - Subject to defined security requirements
        
- Security controls shall be enforced to protect organizational information
    

---

## Awareness & Responsibilities

Remote workers shall:

- Follow information security policies and procedures
    
- Protect devices and information from unauthorized access
    
- Report security incidents or concerns promptly
    

---

## Third- Party & Temporary Remote Work

- Remote access by third parties shall be authorized and controlled
    
- Access shall be limited to defined purposes and durations
    
- Security requirements shall be communicated and enforced
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Management

- Approve remote working arrangements
    
- Support secure remote working practices
    

### IT / Operations

- Implement and maintain secure remote access solutions
    
- Support remote device security
    

### Information Security Function

- Define remote working security requirements
    
- Assess remote working risks
    
- Monitor compliance and incidents
    

### Remote Workers

- Comply with remote working security requirements
    
- Protect organizational information and systems
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to comply with remote working controls may result in:

- Access restrictions
    
- Disciplinary action
    
- Contractual or legal consequences
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued effectiveness of remote working protections
    
- Alignment with evolving work practices and technologies
    
- Compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements
