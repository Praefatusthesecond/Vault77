# Clear Desk and Clear Screen Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure of information by ensuring that sensitive information and systems are protected when not actively in use.

Clear desk and clear screen practices help safeguard information in offices, shared workspaces, remote locations, and public environments.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All employees, contractors, and third parties
    
- All work locations, including offices, home offices, and remote or temporary workspaces
    
- All information assets, regardless of format
    
- All devices used to access organizational information
    

---

## Control Reference

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Annex A 7.7** - Clear desk and clear screen
    

(Standard: **ISO/IEC 27001**)

---

## Policy Statement

Workspaces and information systems shall be secured when unattended to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, or damage to information and associated assets.

Sensitive information shall not be left exposed in physical or digital form when not actively required.

---

## Clear Desk Requirements

Users shall:

- Remove sensitive documents from desks when not in use
    
- Store physical information assets in locked cabinets or drawers where required
    
- Secure removable media when not in use
    
- Dispose of confidential waste using approved secure disposal methods
    

Users shall **not**:

- Leave confidential or restricted information unattended on desks
    
- Store sensitive information in unsecured locations
    

---

## Clear Screen Requirements

Users shall:

- Lock their screens when leaving devices unattended
    
- Use automatic screen locking with a defined inactivity timeout
    
- Ensure screens are positioned to reduce the risk of shoulder surfing
    
- Log out of systems at the end of the workday or when no longer required
    

Users shall **not**:

- Leave systems logged in and unattended
    
- Share active sessions with others
    

---

## Shared & Public Workspaces

- Additional care shall be taken in shared, open, or public environments
    
- Sensitive conversations shall be avoided where they may be overheard
    
- Portable devices shall not be left unattended in public spaces
    

---

## Remote & Home Working

- Clear desk and clear screen practices apply equally to remote work locations
    
- Work devices and information shall be protected from access by unauthorized household members or visitors
    
- Printed sensitive information should be minimized and securely stored
    

---

## Responsibilities

### Users

- Apply clear desk and clear screen practices consistently
    
- Protect information and devices under their control
    
- Report loss, theft, or suspected exposure of information immediately
    

### Management

- Promote awareness and compliance with clear desk and clear screen practices
    
- Support appropriate physical security measures
    

### Information Security Function

- Define clear desk and clear screen requirements
    
- Support awareness and training
    
- Monitor compliance where appropriate
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to comply with clear desk and clear screen controls may result in:

- Increased risk of information disclosure
    
- Disciplinary action
    
- Contractual or legal consequences
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued relevance to working practices
    
- Alignment with physical security and remote working arrangements
    
- Effectiveness based on incident trends or audit findings
