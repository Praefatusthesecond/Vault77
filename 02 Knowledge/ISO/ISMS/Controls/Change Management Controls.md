# Change Management Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that changes to information systems, infrastructure, applications, and configurations are managed in a controlled manner to reduce the risk of unintended disruption, security weaknesses, or non- compliance.

Effective change management helps maintain the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets while enabling controlled improvement.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- Changes to information systems, applications, and infrastructure
    
- Changes to configurations, security controls, and system parameters
    
- Changes to processes supporting information processing
    
- Changes implemented by internal staff or third parties
    

---

## Control Reference

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Annex A 8.32** - Change management
    

(Standard: **ISO/IEC 27001**)

---

## Policy Statement

All changes that may impact information security or service availability shall be formally assessed, authorized, tested, implemented, and documented prior to deployment.

Emergency changes shall be controlled and reviewed retrospectively.

---

## Change Management Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Changes are justified by a business or technical need
    
- Risks associated with changes are identified and assessed
    
- Security impacts are considered before implementation
    
- Changes are traceable from request to completion
    
- Responsibilities are clearly defined
    

---

## Change Classification

Changes may be categorized, for example, as:

- **Standard changes** - Low risk, pre- approved, routine changes
    
- **Normal changes** - Require assessment, approval, and testing
    
- **Emergency changes** - Urgent changes to resolve incidents or critical risks
    

The level of control applied shall be proportionate to the risk and impact of the change.

---

## Change Request & Approval

- Changes shall be formally requested and documented
    
- Each change shall include:
    
    - Description and rationale
        
    - Impact and risk assessment
        
    - Security considerations
        
    - Rollback or recovery plan
        
- Changes shall be approved by appropriate authority prior to implementation
    

---

## Testing & Validation

- Changes shall be tested prior to deployment, where feasible
    
- Testing shall verify:
    
    - Functional correctness
        
    - Security requirements
        
    - Compatibility with existing systems
        
- Test results shall be documented
    

---

## Change Implementation

- Approved changes shall be implemented according to plan
    
- Changes shall be scheduled to minimize business impact
    
- Implementation activities shall be logged where appropriate
    

---

## Emergency Changes

- Emergency changes may bypass standard approval steps where necessary
    
- Emergency changes shall be:
    
    - Authorized by designated personnel
        
    - Documented as soon as possible
        
    - Reviewed after implementation to assess risk and impact
        

---

## Documentation & Traceability

- Records of changes shall be retained, including:
    
    - Requests
        
    - Approvals
        
    - Testing results
        
    - Implementation details
        
- Changes shall be traceable for audit and investigation purposes
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Change Requestors

- Provide accurate and complete change information
    
- Assess potential impacts and risks
    

### Approvers

- Evaluate risks and benefits
    
- Ensure security considerations are addressed
    

### IT / Operations

- Implement and test approved changes
    
- Maintain change records
    

### Information Security Function

- Review security- relevant changes
    
- Monitor compliance with change management requirements
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to comply with change management controls may result in:

- Service disruption
    
- Introduction of security vulnerabilities
    
- Disciplinary or contractual consequences
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued effectiveness
    
- Alignment with business and security requirements
    
- Integration with incident and problem management processes
