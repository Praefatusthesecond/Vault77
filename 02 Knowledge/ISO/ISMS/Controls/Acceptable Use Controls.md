# Acceptable Use of Information & Assets

## Purpose

The purpose of this control is to ensure that information and other associated assets are used in a responsible, secure, and compliant manner, in line with the organization’s information security objectives and the requirements of **ISO/IEC 27001 (2022)**.

Clear acceptable use rules reduce the risk of misuse, data leakage, security incidents, and legal or regulatory non- compliance.

---

## Scope

This control applies to:

- All employees, contractors, interns, and third parties with access to organizational assets
    
- All information assets, regardless of format (digital, physical, verbal)
    
- All systems, services, devices, and networks owned, leased, or managed by the organization
    

---

## Control Reference

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Annex A 5.10**  
    _Acceptable use of information and other associated assets_
    

---

## Policy Statement

Information and associated assets shall only be used for authorized business purposes and in accordance with defined acceptable use rules. Users are responsible for protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the assets entrusted to them.

Misuse, unauthorized use, or negligent handling of information assets is prohibited.

---

## Acceptable Use Rules

### General Principles

Users shall:

- Use information assets only for legitimate business purposes
    
- Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, contracts, and internal policies
    
- Protect assets against unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or destruction
    
- Follow the principle of least privilege when accessing systems and data
    

---

### Information Assets

Users shall:

- Access, process, store, and transmit information only when authorized
    
- Handle information according to its classification (e.g. public, internal, confidential)
    
- Avoid copying, sharing, or exporting information unless explicitly permitted
    
- Ensure sensitive information is not exposed in public or insecure environments
    

Users shall **not**:

- Circumvent security controls
    
- Access information outside their role or authorization
    
- Share credentials or authentication mechanisms
    

---

### IT Systems & Devices

Users shall:

- Use organizational devices and systems in accordance with documented procedures
    
- Keep systems protected using approved security controls (e.g. passwords, MFA)
    
- Lock devices when unattended
    
- Report loss, theft, or suspected compromise immediately
    

Users shall **not**:

- Install unauthorized software or hardware
    
- Disable or bypass security mechanisms
    
- Use systems for illegal, unethical, or unauthorized activities
    

---

### Network & Internet Usage

Users shall:

- Use network and internet access responsibly and in support of business activities
    
- Be aware that network usage may be logged and monitored for security purposes
    

Users shall **not**:

- Access malicious, illegal, or inappropriate content
    
- Introduce malware or malicious code
    
- Use organizational networks to attack or probe external systems
    

---

### Third- Party & Remote Access

Users shall:

- Use remote access solutions approved by the organization
    
- Ensure third- party access is limited to agreed purposes and durations
    
- Protect organizational assets when working remotely or off- site
    

---

## Responsibilities

### Users

- Understand and comply with acceptable use requirements
    
- Protect assets under their control
    
- Promptly report security incidents or suspected misuse
    

### Management

- Ensure acceptable use rules are communicated and enforced
    
- Support awareness and training initiatives
    

### Information Security Function

- Define, maintain, and review acceptable use rules
    
- Monitor compliance and investigate violations
    

---

## Awareness & Acknowledgement

All users shall be informed of acceptable use requirements and may be required to formally acknowledge their understanding and acceptance.

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Violation of acceptable use rules may result in:

- Access revocation
    
- Disciplinary action
    
- Contractual consequences
    
- Legal action, where applicable
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

This control shall be reviewed periodically and updated as required to reflect:

- Changes in business operations
    
- Changes in technology
    
- Updates to legal, regulatory, or ISO requirements
