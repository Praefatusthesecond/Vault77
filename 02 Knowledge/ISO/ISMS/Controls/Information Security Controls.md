# Information Security Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve an effective framework for managing information security risks and protecting information assets.

Information security controls ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information in alignment with business objectives, legal and regulatory requirements, and risk tolerance.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- The entire Information Security Management System (ISMS)
    
- All information assets within the ISMS scope
    
- All organizational units, processes, systems, and services
    
- All employees, contractors, and third parties with access to information
    

---

## Policy Statement

The organization shall implement appropriate organizational, people, physical, and technological information security controls based on risk assessment and treatment to protect information assets against threats and vulnerabilities.

Information security controls shall be documented, implemented, monitored, reviewed, and continually improved.

---

## Information Security Objectives

Information security controls shall support the following objectives:

- Protect information from unauthorized access or disclosure
    
- Prevent unauthorized modification or destruction of information
    
- Ensure information and systems are available when required
    
- Maintain trust with customers, partners, and stakeholders
    
- Meet legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations
    

---

## Control Framework

Information security controls are structured and applied across the following domains:

### Organizational Controls

- Policies, procedures, and governance structures
    
- Asset management
    
- Supplier and third- party security
    
- Incident and continuity management
    

### People Controls

- Awareness and training
    
- Acceptable use
    
- Clear desk and clear screen
    
- Human resource security
    

### Physical Controls

- Physical access controls
    
- Protection of facilities and equipment
    

### Technological Controls

- Access control and identity management
    
- Cryptographic and encryption controls
    
- Backup and recovery
    
- Logging, monitoring, and secure configuration
    

---

## Risk- Based Control Selection

- Information security controls shall be selected based on:
    
    - Risk assessments
        
    - Business requirements
        
    - Information classification
        
    - Legal and regulatory obligations
        
- Controls shall be proportionate to the identified risks
    
- Residual risks shall be accepted or treated in accordance with defined criteria
    

---

## Implementation & Integration

- Information security controls shall be:
    
    - Documented and approved
        
    - Integrated into business processes and systems
        
    - Communicated to relevant stakeholders
        
- Controls shall be implemented consistently across the organization
    

---

## Monitoring & Measurement

- The effectiveness of information security controls shall be monitored
    
- Metrics and indicators may be used to:
    
    - Assess control performance
        
    - Identify trends and weaknesses
        
- Results shall be reviewed and used to drive improvements
    

---

## Incident Handling & Control Failures

- Failures or weaknesses in information security controls shall be:
    
    - Identified
        
    - Reported
        
    - Investigated
        
- Control failures may result in corrective actions, improvements, or risk reassessment
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Management

- Provide leadership and support for information security
    
- Approve policies and objectives
    
- Ensure adequate resources
    

### Information Security Function

- Define and maintain information security controls
    
- Monitor compliance and effectiveness
    
- Coordinate improvement activities
    

### Asset & Process Owners

- Ensure controls are applied within their areas of responsibility
    

### All Personnel

- Comply with information security controls
    
- Protect information assets
    
- Report security incidents or weaknesses
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Non- compliance with information security controls may result in:

- Increased security risk
    
- Disciplinary measures
    
- Contractual or legal consequences
    

---

## Review & Continual Improvement

Information security controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued suitability and effectiveness
    
- Alignment with business objectives and risks
    
- Incorporation of lessons learned and best practices
