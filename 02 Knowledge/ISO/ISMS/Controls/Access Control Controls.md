# Access Control

## Purpose

The purpose of this control is to ensure that access to information and other associated assets is restricted to authorized users, processes, and systems only, in accordance with business and security requirements.

Effective access control reduces the risk of unauthorized access, data breaches, and misuse of information assets.

---

## Scope

This control applies to:

- All users (employees, contractors, third parties)
    
- All information systems, applications, networks, and services
    
- All information assets, regardless of classification or format

## Policy Statement

Access to information and associated assets shall be granted based on business need, using the principles of **least privilege** and **need- to- know**. Access rights shall be formally authorized, documented, reviewed, and revoked when no longer required.

---

## Access Control Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Access is granted only to authorized identities
    
- Users are uniquely identifiable where required
    
- Privileged access is tightly controlled and monitored
    
- Access rights align with job roles and responsibilities
    
- Segregation of duties is enforced where feasible
    

---

## Identity Management

- Each user shall be assigned a unique identity
    
- Shared or generic accounts are prohibited unless explicitly approved and justified
    
- User identities shall be lifecycle- managed (creation, modification, deactivation)
    
- Identity records shall be kept accurate and up to date
    

Identity provisioning and deprovisioning shall follow documented procedures.

---

## Authentication

- Authentication mechanisms shall be appropriate to the sensitivity of the system or data
    
- Strong authentication methods (e.g. MFA) shall be used where required
    
- Authentication credentials shall be protected against disclosure and misuse
    

Users shall **not**:

- Share authentication credentials
    
- Reuse organizational credentials on external systems
    
- Circumvent authentication mechanisms
    

---

## Authorization & Access Rights

- Access rights shall be granted based on approved requests
    
- Role- based access control (RBAC) should be used where possible
    
- Privileged access shall be restricted to a limited number of authorized users
    
- Temporary or elevated access shall be time- bound and reviewed
    

---

## Review of Access Rights

- Access rights shall be reviewed at regular intervals
    
- Reviews shall also occur upon:
    
    - Role or responsibility changes
        
    - Employment termination
        
    - Contract expiration
        

Unauthorized or unnecessary access shall be promptly removed.

---

## Privileged Access Management

- Administrative and privileged accounts shall be strictly controlled
    
- Privileged activities shall be logged and monitored
    
- Use of privileged access shall be limited to authorized tasks only
    

---

## Access Control for Third Parties

- Third- party access shall be limited to contractual and business requirements
    
- Access shall be approved, documented, and time- bound
    
- Third- party access shall be revoked immediately when no longer required
    

---

## Responsibilities

### Users

- Use access rights responsibly and only for authorized purposes
    
- Protect authentication information
    
- Report suspected unauthorized access immediately
    

### Management

- Approve access based on business needs
    
- Support periodic access reviews
    

### Information Security Function

- Define access control rules and standards
    
- Monitor compliance and investigate violations
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to comply with access control requirements may result in:

- Access revocation
    
- Disciplinary action
    
- Contractual or legal consequences
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

This control shall be reviewed periodically to ensure alignment with:

- Organizational changes
    
- Technology changes
    
- Legal and regulatory requirements
    
- ISO/IEC 27001 updates
