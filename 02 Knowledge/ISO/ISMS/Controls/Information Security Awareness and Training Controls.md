# Information Security Awareness and Training Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that personnel are aware of information security risks, responsibilities, and required behaviors, and that they possess the necessary competence to perform their roles securely.

Effective awareness and training reduce the likelihood of human error, policy violations, and security incidents.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All employees, contractors, interns, and temporary staff
    
- Third parties with access to organizational information or systems
    
- All roles within the scope of the Information Security Management System (ISMS)
    

---

## Control References

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Clause 7.2** - Competence
    
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Clause 7.3** - Awareness
    

(Standard: **ISO/IEC 27001**)

---

## Policy Statement

All personnel shall receive appropriate information security awareness and training to ensure they understand their responsibilities and can perform their duties in a secure and compliant manner.

Awareness and training activities shall be proportionate to role, risk, and access to information assets.

---

## Awareness Objectives

Information security awareness activities shall ensure that personnel understand:

- The importance of information security
    
- Relevant information security policies and procedures
    
- Their individual responsibilities for protecting information
    
- How to recognize and report security incidents
    
- The consequences of non- compliance
    

---

## Training Requirements

### General Awareness Training

- All personnel shall receive baseline information security awareness training
    
- Training shall cover topics such as:
    
    - Acceptable use
        
    - Password and authentication practices
        
    - Phishing and social engineering
        
    - Data protection and classification
        
    - Incident reporting
        

---

### Role- Based Training

- Additional training shall be provided for roles with increased security responsibility, such as:
    
    - IT and system administrators
        
    - Developers
        
    - Managers and asset owners
        
- Role- based training shall address specific risks and controls relevant to the role
    

---

### Specialized Training

- Specialized training shall be provided where required, such as:
    
    - Secure development practices
        
    - Incident response procedures
        
    - Regulatory or compliance obligations
        

---

## Training Delivery & Frequency

- Awareness and training may be delivered through:
    
    - Instructor- led sessions
        
    - E- learning platforms
        
    - Workshops or simulations
        
    - Awareness campaigns
        
- Awareness activities shall be conducted periodically
    
- Training shall be refreshed at planned intervals and upon significant change
    

---

## Onboarding & Changes

- Information security awareness shall be included in onboarding processes
    
- Additional training shall be provided when:
    
    - Roles or responsibilities change
        
    - New systems or significant risks are introduced
        

---

## Awareness Reinforcement

- Ongoing awareness may be reinforced through:
    
    - Communications and reminders
        
    - Posters or internal messaging
        
    - Phishing simulations or exercises
        
- Awareness materials shall remain relevant and up to date
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Management

- Promote a culture of information security awareness
    
- Ensure resources for training and awareness activities
    

### Information Security Function

- Define awareness and training requirements
    
- Develop or approve training content
    
- Monitor effectiveness of awareness activities
    

### Human Resources

- Support onboarding and role- based training processes
    
- Maintain training records
    

### Personnel

- Participate in required awareness and training activities
    
- Apply learned practices in daily work
    

---

## Records & Evidence

- Records of training and awareness activities shall be maintained
    
- Records may include:
    
    - Attendance logs
        
    - Completion certificates
        
    - Training materials
        
- Records shall be retained in accordance with retention requirements
    

---

## Monitoring & Effectiveness

- Effectiveness of awareness and training shall be evaluated through:
    
    - Assessments or quizzes
        
    - Incident trends
        
    - Audit findings
        
- Results shall be used to improve awareness and training programs
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to complete required training or comply with awareness requirements may result in:

- Access restrictions
    
- Disciplinary action
    
- Contractual consequences
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued relevance to risks and roles
    
- Effectiveness of awareness and training activities
    
- Alignment with ISMS objectives
