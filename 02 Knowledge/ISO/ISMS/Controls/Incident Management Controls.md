# Incident Management Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that information security incidents are identified, reported, assessed, responded to, and resolved in a consistent and timely manner, minimizing impact to the organization.

Effective incident management supports rapid containment, recovery, learning, and continual improvement of information security.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All information security incidents and suspected incidents
    
- All employees, contractors, and third parties
    
- All systems, applications, networks, and information assets
    
- Incidents occurring internally or involving third parties

## Policy Statement

Information security incidents and events shall be reported, assessed, managed, and resolved in a timely, structured, and coordinated manner.

The organization shall learn from incidents to reduce the likelihood and impact of future occurrences.

---

## Incident Management Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Personnel know how and when to report incidents
    
- Security events are assessed consistently
    
- Incidents are handled proportionate to their severity and impact
    
- Responsibilities and escalation paths are clearly defined
    
- Lessons learned are captured and acted upon
    

---

## Definition of Events and Incidents

- **Information security event**: An observed occurrence that may affect information security
    
- **Information security incident**: One or more events that compromise, or threaten to compromise, confidentiality, integrity, or availability
    

Events shall be assessed to determine whether they constitute incidents.

---

## Incident Identification & Reporting

- All personnel shall promptly report:
    
    - Suspected or confirmed security incidents
        
    - Unusual system behavior
        
    - Data loss or disclosure
        
- Reporting channels shall be clearly defined and communicated
    
- No disciplinary action shall result from good- faith reporting
    

---

## Incident Assessment & Classification

- Reported events shall be assessed to determine:
    
    - Severity
        
    - Impact
        
    - Urgency
        
- Incidents shall be classified (e.g. low / medium / high / critical)
    
- Classification shall guide response and escalation
    

---

## Incident Response

Incident response activities may include:

- Containment of the incident
    
- Eradication of the root cause
    
- Recovery of systems and data
    
- Communication with relevant stakeholders
    

Response actions shall be documented and coordinated.

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Incident Response Team / Responsible Roles

- Coordinate incident handling activities
    
- Ensure timely response and escalation
    

### IT / Operations

- Provide technical support for containment and recovery
    
- Preserve system integrity
    

### Information Security Function

- Lead incident management process
    
- Assess security impact
    
- Coordinate communication and reporting
    

### Management

- Provide decision- making support
    
- Approve major response actions
    

### Users

- Report incidents promptly
    
- Cooperate with investigations
    

---

## Communication & Escalation

- Incident communication paths shall be defined
    
- Escalation shall occur based on severity and impact
    
- External communication shall be controlled and authorized
    

---

## Evidence Collection & Preservation

- Evidence shall be collected where required for:
    
    - Investigation
        
    - Legal or regulatory purposes
        
- Evidence handling shall preserve integrity and chain of custody
    

---

## Incident Logging & Documentation

- Incidents shall be recorded, including:
    
    - Description
        
    - Timeline
        
    - Actions taken
        
    - Outcomes
        
- Records shall support audits and improvement activities
    

---

## Learning & Improvement

- Incidents shall be reviewed after resolution
    
- Lessons learned shall be identified and documented
    
- Improvements may include:
    
    - Control enhancements
        
    - Process updates
        
    - Training or awareness improvements
        

---

## Third- Party Incidents

- Suppliers shall be required to report relevant incidents
    
- Third- party incidents affecting the organization shall be handled under these controls
    
- Contractual obligations shall be enforced
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to report or properly manage incidents may result in:

- Increased impact or damage
    
- Regulatory or contractual consequences
    
- Disciplinary action
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

Incident management controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued effectiveness
    
- Alignment with threat landscape
    
- Integration with monitoring, risk management, and business continuity
