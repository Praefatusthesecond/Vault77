# Logging and Monitoring Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that security- relevant events are logged, monitored, and reviewed to detect unauthorized activities, security incidents, system failures, and policy violations in a timely manner.

Effective logging and monitoring support incident detection, investigation, compliance, and continual improvement of information security.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All systems, applications, networks, and infrastructure within ISMS scope
    
- On- premises, cloud, and third- party hosted environments
    
- Security- relevant events, activities, and system behaviors
    
- Logs generated by users, administrators, applications, and security controls
    

---

## Control References

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Annex A 8.15** - Logging
    
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Annex A 8.16** - Monitoring activities
    

(Standard: **ISO/IEC 27001**)

---

## Policy Statement

Security- relevant events shall be logged and monitored to enable timely detection of threats, investigation of incidents, and verification of control effectiveness.

Logging and monitoring activities shall be proportionate to risk, system criticality, and information sensitivity.

---

## Logging Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Logs provide sufficient detail to support investigation and auditing
    
- Logs are accurate, complete, and protected against tampering
    
- Logging is enabled by default on critical systems
    
- Logging activities respect legal, regulatory, and privacy requirements
    

---

## Events to Be Logged

Security- relevant events may include, where applicable:

- User authentication and authorization events
    
- Privileged access and administrative actions
    
- System configuration changes
    
- Access to sensitive or restricted data
    
- Security control alerts and failures
    
- Application and system errors
    
- Network and firewall events
    

The specific events logged shall be defined based on risk and system role.

---

## Log Protection & Integrity

- Logs shall be protected against:
    
    - Unauthorized access
        
    - Modification or deletion
        
    - Loss or destruction
        
- Access to logs shall be restricted based on least privilege
    
- Log integrity mechanisms shall be used where appropriate
    

---

## Centralized Logging

- Logs from critical systems shall be centralized where feasible
    
- Centralized logging shall support:
    
    - Correlation of events
        
    - Efficient monitoring and analysis
        
    - Incident investigation
        

---

## Monitoring Activities

- Logged events shall be monitored to identify:
    
    - Suspicious behavior
        
    - Policy violations
        
    - Indicators of compromise
        
    - Operational failures
        
- Monitoring may be:
    
    - Automated
        
    - Manual
        
    - Risk- based
        

---

## Alerting & Response

- Alerts shall be generated for significant or high- risk events
    
- Alert thresholds and escalation paths shall be defined
    
- Monitoring outputs shall feed into incident management processes
    

---

## Log Retention

- Log retention periods shall be defined and documented
    
- Retention shall consider:
    
    - Legal and regulatory requirements
        
    - Investigation and audit needs
        
    - Storage and performance considerations
        
- Logs shall be securely deleted or archived when retention periods expire
    

---

## Privacy & Legal Considerations

- Logging and monitoring shall comply with applicable privacy and labor regulations
    
- Monitoring shall be proportionate and transparent where required
    
- Access to personal data within logs shall be restricted and justified
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### System & Service Owners

- Define logging requirements for their systems
    
- Ensure logging is enabled and appropriate
    

### IT / Operations

- Implement and maintain logging and monitoring solutions
    
- Ensure log collection, storage, and availability
    

### Information Security Function

- Define logging and monitoring standards
    
- Review monitoring outputs and trends
    
- Support incident investigation and improvement activities
    

---

## Monitoring Effectiveness

- Logging and monitoring effectiveness shall be reviewed periodically
    
- Reviews may consider:
    
    - Incident detection performance
        
    - False positives and missed events
        
    - Audit findings
        
- Improvements shall be implemented where gaps are identified
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to comply with logging and monitoring controls may result in:

- Reduced ability to detect incidents
    
- Increased security risk
    
- Audit findings or compliance issues
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued effectiveness
    
- Alignment with evolving threats and technologies
    
- Compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements
