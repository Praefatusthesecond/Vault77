# Information Classification and Handling Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that information is classified and handled according to its value, sensitivity, and criticality, in order to protect it from unauthorized disclosure, modification, loss, or destruction.

Consistent classification and handling enable appropriate security controls to be applied throughout the information lifecycle.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All information created, processed, stored, or transmitted by the organization
    
- Information in all forms, including digital, physical, and verbal
    
- Information handled by employees, contractors, and third parties
    
- On- premises, cloud, remote, and third- party environments
    

---

---

## Policy Statement

Information shall be classified based on its sensitivity, value, and criticality, and handled in accordance with defined classification and handling requirements throughout its lifecycle.

Classification and handling rules shall be clearly communicated and consistently applied.

---

## Information Classification Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Information is classified in a consistent and understandable manner
    
- Classification reflects confidentiality, integrity, and availability requirements
    
- Protection measures are proportionate to risk
    
- Classification is reviewed and updated when information or context changes
    

---

## Classification Levels

Classification levels shall be defined and documented.  
Typical examples may include:

- **Public** - Information approved for public disclosure
    
- **Internal** - Information intended for internal use only
    
- **Confidential** - Sensitive business or personal information
    
- **Restricted** - Highly sensitive or critical information
    

The number and naming of levels may be adapted to organizational needs.

---

## Information Ownership

- Each information asset shall have a designated owner
    
- Information owners are responsible for:
    
    - Assigning and reviewing classification
        
    - Defining handling requirements
        
    - Approving access and sharing
        

---

## Labelling of Information

- Information shall be labelled where appropriate to reflect its classification
    
- Labelling may be applied:
    
    - Digitally (metadata, headers, footers)
        
    - Physically (paper documents, media labels)
        
- Where labelling is not feasible, alternative measures shall be applied
    

---

## Information Handling Requirements

Handling requirements shall be defined based on classification and may include controls for:

### Storage

- Approved storage locations and systems
    
- Encryption or physical protection requirements
    

### Access

- Access restrictions based on need- to- know
    
- Monitoring of access to sensitive information
    

### Transmission

- Secure transmission mechanisms
    
- Restrictions on external sharing
    

### Processing

- Approved tools and environments
    
- Protection against unauthorized modification
    

---

## Handling in Different Environments

### Physical Environments

- Secure storage of paper documents and removable media
    
- Controlled access to offices and storage areas
    

### Digital Environments

- Logical access controls
    
- Encryption where required
    
- Secure configuration of systems
    

### Remote & Mobile Working

- Additional safeguards for portable devices
    
- Protection against unauthorized viewing or access
    

---

## Information Sharing & Disclosure

- Information sharing shall be:
    
    - Authorized
        
    - Purpose- limited
        
    - Proportionate to risk
        
- Sharing with third parties shall comply with contractual and security requirements
    

---

## Reclassification & Declassification

- Information classification shall be reviewed periodically
    
- Reclassification shall occur when:
    
    - Information sensitivity changes
        
    - Business or legal requirements change
        
- Declassification shall be documented where appropriate
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Information Owners

- Classify and review information
    
- Define handling requirements
    

### Users

- Handle information according to its classification
    
- Apply labelling and protection measures
    
- Report suspected misclassification or mishandling
    

### Information Security Function

- Define classification and handling standards
    
- Support awareness and training
    
- Monitor compliance
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to comply with classification and handling requirements may result in:

- Increased risk of information disclosure
    
- Disciplinary action
    
- Contractual or legal consequences
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued alignment with business and risk context
    
- Effectiveness of classification and handling rules
    
- Consistency with other ISMS controls
