# Risk Management Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that information security risks are systematically identified, analyzed, evaluated, treated, monitored, and reviewed to protect information assets and support informed decision- making.

Effective risk management ensures that information security controls are proportionate, justified, and aligned with business objectives and risk appetite.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- The entire Information Security Management System (ISMS)
    
- All information assets within ISMS scope
    
- All organizational units, processes, systems, and services
    
- Risks arising from internal and external contexts, including third parties

## Policy Statement

The organization shall establish and maintain a risk management process to identify, assess, and treat information security risks in a consistent, repeatable, and documented manner.

Risk management activities shall be integrated into business processes and used as the basis for selecting and prioritizing information security controls.

---

## Risk Management Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Risks are identified and managed proactively
    
- Risk assessments are repeatable and comparable over time
    
- Risk treatment decisions are justified and documented
    
- Residual risks are explicitly accepted or treated
    
- Risk management supports continual improvement
    

---

## Risk Identification

- Information security risks shall be identified based on:
    
    - Assets and their value
        
    - Threats and vulnerabilities
        
    - Business processes and dependencies
        
- Risks may arise from:
    
    - Technology
        
    - People
        
    - Processes
        
    - Physical and environmental factors
        
    - Third parties and supply chain
        

---

## Risk Analysis

- Identified risks shall be analyzed to determine:
    
    - Likelihood of occurrence
        
    - Potential impact on confidentiality, integrity, and availability
        
- Analysis methods and criteria shall be defined and documented
    

---

## Risk Evaluation

- Risks shall be evaluated against defined risk acceptance criteria
    
- Risks shall be prioritized to support decision- making
    
- Unacceptable risks shall be treated
    

---

## Risk Treatment

Risk treatment options may include:

- **Risk mitigation** - Implementing or strengthening controls
    
- **Risk avoidance** - Discontinuing risk- generating activities
    
- **Risk transfer** - Sharing risk (e.g. insurance, contracts)
    
- **Risk acceptance** - Accepting residual risk with approval
    

Risk treatment decisions shall be documented in a risk treatment plan.

---

## Selection of Controls

- Controls shall be selected based on:
    
    - Risk treatment decisions
        
    - Annex A controls
        
    - Legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements
        
- Deviations from Annex A shall be justified and documented
    

---

## Risk Acceptance

- Residual risks shall be:
    
    - Clearly documented
        
    - Formally approved by authorized management
        
- Accepted risks shall be reviewed periodically
    

---

## Risk Monitoring & Review

- Risks shall be reviewed at planned intervals
    
- Reviews shall also occur upon:
    
    - Significant changes to systems or processes
        
    - Security incidents
        
    - Changes in threat landscape
        
- Risk status shall be updated accordingly
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Management

- Define risk appetite and acceptance criteria
    
- Approve significant risks and treatment decisions
    

### Information Security Function

- Define and maintain the risk management methodology
    
- Facilitate risk assessments and reviews
    
- Maintain the risk register
    

### Asset & Process Owners

- Identify and assess risks related to their areas
    
- Implement and maintain risk treatment measures
    

### All Personnel

- Contribute to risk identification
    
- Follow risk treatment controls
    

---

## Documentation & Records

- Risk management activities shall be documented, including:
    
    - Risk assessment methodology
        
    - Risk register
        
    - Risk treatment plans
        
    - Risk acceptance records
        

---

## Compliance & Effectiveness

Failure to maintain effective risk management may result in:

- Inadequate or misaligned controls
    
- Increased exposure to security incidents
    
- Audit findings or nonconformities
    

---

## Review & Continual Improvement

Risk management controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued suitability and effectiveness
    
- Alignment with business objectives
    
- Integration with continual improvement processes
