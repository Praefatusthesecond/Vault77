# Business Continuity Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that information security is maintained during disruptive events and that information and communication technology (ICT) systems are adequately prepared to support the continuation and recovery of critical business processes.

Business continuity controls reduce the impact of incidents and ensure the timely restoration of essential services.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All critical business processes
    
- Information assets supporting those processes
    
- ICT systems, applications, networks, and infrastructure
    
- Personnel and third parties involved in business continuity and recovery activities
    

---

## Control References

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Annex A 5.29** - Information security during disruption
    
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Annex A 5.30** - ICT readiness for business continuity
    

(Standard: **ISO/IEC 27001**)

---

## Policy Statement

The organization shall establish, implement, and maintain business continuity arrangements to ensure that information security and critical ICT services are maintained or restored within acceptable timeframes following a disruptive event.

Business continuity planning shall be aligned with business objectives, risk assessments, and information security requirements.

---

## Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

- A Business Impact Analysis shall be performed to:
    
    - Identify critical business processes
        
    - Assess the impact of disruptions
        
    - Define recovery priorities
        
- Dependencies on information, systems, personnel, and third parties shall be identified
    
- BIA results shall be reviewed periodically and upon significant change
    

---

## Continuity & Recovery Objectives

- Recovery objectives shall be defined for critical processes and systems, including:
    
    - Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)
        
    - Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)
        
- Objectives shall be aligned with business requirements and risk tolerance
    

---

## Business Continuity Planning

- Business continuity plans (BCPs) shall be documented and maintained
    
- Plans shall address:
    
    - Disruption scenarios
        
    - Roles and responsibilities
        
    - Communication procedures
        
    - Manual or alternative processing where applicable
        
- Information security requirements shall be integrated into all continuity plans
    

---

## ICT Readiness for Business Continuity

- ICT systems supporting critical processes shall be designed for resilience
    
- Measures may include:
    
    - Redundancy
        
    - Failover mechanisms
        
    - Backup and restoration capabilities
        
    - Capacity planning
        
- Continuity measures shall be proportionate to system criticality
    

---

## Information Security During Disruption

- Security controls shall remain effective during disruptive events
    
- Temporary or emergency access shall be:
    
    - Authorized
        
    - Logged
        
    - Reviewed after the event
        
- Confidentiality, integrity, and availability requirements shall be maintained wherever possible
    

---

## Testing & Exercising

- Business continuity and recovery plans shall be tested at planned intervals
    
- Testing may include:
    
    - Tabletop exercises
        
    - Technical recovery tests
        
    - Simulation of disruption scenarios
        
- Test results shall be documented and used to improve plans
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Management

- Approve business continuity strategies
    
- Ensure adequate resources for continuity planning
    

### Process & System Owners

- Define continuity requirements for their areas
    
- Participate in testing and reviews
    

### IT / Operations

- Implement ICT continuity measures
    
- Execute recovery procedures during incidents
    

### Information Security Function

- Ensure alignment between business continuity and information security
    
- Review continuity controls for effectiveness
    

---

## Third- Party & Supply Chain Considerations

- Business continuity requirements shall be considered when engaging third parties
    
- Dependencies on external services shall be identified and documented
    
- Third- party continuity capabilities shall be assessed where relevant
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to maintain adequate business continuity controls may result in:

- Prolonged service disruption
    
- Increased security risk
    
- Regulatory or contractual consequences
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Alignment with business objectives
    
- Continued effectiveness following organizational or technological changes
    
- Lessons learned from incidents and tests are incorporated
