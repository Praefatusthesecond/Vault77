# Data Retention Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that data is retained only for as long as necessary to meet business, legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements, and is securely disposed of when no longer required.

Effective data retention controls reduce legal risk, limit exposure in case of incidents, and support data protection and privacy principles.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All data created, processed, stored, or managed by the organization
    
- Structured and unstructured data
    
- Personal, confidential, and business- critical information
    
- Data stored in on- premises, cloud, backup, and third- party systems

## Policy Statement

Data shall be retained only for defined and approved periods that are justified by business, legal, regulatory, or contractual requirements.

Once retention periods expire, data shall be securely deleted or destroyed in a manner appropriate to its classification and sensitivity.

---

## Data Retention Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Retention periods are clearly defined and documented
    
- Data is not retained longer than necessary
    
- Retention requirements are based on risk, legal, and business needs
    
- Retention and disposal processes are consistently applied
    
- Retention controls are auditable and enforceable
    

---

## Definition of Retention Periods

- Retention periods shall be defined for data categories, such as:
    
    - Operational data
        
    - Financial and accounting records
        
    - Personnel data
        
    - Customer or client data
        
    - Logs and monitoring data
        
    - Backup data
        
- Retention periods shall consider:
    
    - Legal and regulatory obligations
        
    - Contractual requirements
        
    - Business needs
        
    - Risk assessments
        

Retention requirements shall be reviewed periodically.

---

## Data Storage During Retention

- Data shall be stored securely throughout its retention period
    
- Protection measures shall align with data classification
    
- Access to retained data shall remain restricted and monitored
    
- Archived data shall be protected against unauthorized access or alteration
    

---

## Backup & Retention Alignment

- Backup retention periods shall be defined and documented
    
- Backup retention shall align with data retention requirements where feasible
    
- Special consideration shall be given to:
    
    - Long- term backups
        
    - Immutable or off- site backups
        

---

## Secure Disposal & Destruction

- Data shall be securely deleted or destroyed once retention periods expire
    
- Disposal methods shall prevent reconstruction or recovery of data
    
- Secure disposal methods may include:
    
    - Logical deletion
        
    - Cryptographic erasure
        
    - Physical destruction of media
        
- Disposal activities shall be documented where appropriate
    

---

## Legal Hold & Retention Exceptions

- Retention periods may be suspended due to:
    
    - Legal hold
        
    - Litigation
        
    - Investigations
        
- Exceptions shall be:
    
    - Formally approved
        
    - Documented
        
    - Reviewed periodically
        

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Data Owners

- Define and approve retention requirements for their data
    
- Ensure retention aligns with business and legal needs
    

### Users

- Handle and store data according to retention requirements
    
- Avoid unnecessary duplication or prolonged storage
    

### IT / Operations

- Implement technical retention and deletion mechanisms
    
- Ensure retention policies are enforced across systems
    

### Information Security Function

- Define data retention standards
    
- Monitor compliance
    
- Support audits and corrective actions
    

---

## Monitoring & Compliance

- Compliance with data retention controls shall be monitored
    
- Non- compliance shall be addressed through corrective actions
    
- Retention and disposal activities shall support audit and regulatory reviews
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued compliance with legal and regulatory requirements
    
- Alignment with business needs and risks
    
- Effectiveness of retention and disposal mechanisms
