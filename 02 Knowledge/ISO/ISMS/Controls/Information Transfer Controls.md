# Information Transfer Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that information transferred within the organization and to external parties is protected against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

Effective information transfer controls reduce the risk of data leakage and ensure that information is exchanged securely and appropriately.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All forms of information transfer
    
- Digital, physical, and verbal information exchanges
    
- Internal and external information transfers
    
- Transfers involving employees, contractors, and third parties
    
- On- premises, cloud, and remote working environments
    

---

## Control Reference

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Annex A 5.14** - Information transfer
    

(Standard: **ISO/IEC 27001**)

---

## Policy Statement

Information shall be transferred in a manner that maintains its confidentiality, integrity, and availability, in accordance with its classification and applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.

Information transfer methods and controls shall be appropriate to the sensitivity and risk associated with the information being exchanged.

---

## Information Transfer Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Information is transferred only for authorized and legitimate purposes
    
- Transfer methods are appropriate to the information classification
    
- Information is protected during transmission and delivery
    
- Responsibilities for information protection are clearly defined
    

---

## Approved Transfer Methods

- Approved methods for information transfer shall be defined and documented
    
- Transfer methods may include:
    
    - Secure electronic communication channels
        
    - Encrypted file transfer mechanisms
        
    - Approved collaboration platforms
        
    - Secure physical media transfer
        

Unapproved or insecure transfer methods shall not be used for sensitive information.

---

## Information Classification & Transfer

- Information shall be classified prior to transfer
    
- Classification shall determine:
    
    - Whether transfer is permitted
        
    - Approved transfer methods
        
    - Required protection measures (e.g. encryption)
        
- Highly sensitive information may require additional authorization prior to transfer
    

---

## Secure Electronic Transfer

- Information transferred electronically shall be protected against interception or alteration
    
- Secure communication protocols shall be used where required
    
- Authentication and access controls shall be applied to transfer mechanisms
    

---

## Physical Media Transfer

- Physical media containing information shall be protected during transport
    
- Media shall be:
    
    - Appropriately labelled where required
        
    - Tracked and controlled
        
    - Protected against loss or theft
        
- Secure disposal shall be applied after use
    

---

## Verbal Information Transfer

- Sensitive information shared verbally shall be protected against unauthorized disclosure
    
- Care shall be taken in public or shared environments
    
- Verbal disclosure shall be limited to authorized recipients
    

---

## Information Transfer to Third Parties

- Transfers to third parties shall be:
    
    - Authorized
        
    - Documented
        
    - Limited to defined purposes
        
- Information transfer requirements shall be addressed in contracts or agreements
    
- Third- party recipients shall implement appropriate protection measures
    

---

## Information Receipt & Verification

- Received information shall be verified where appropriate to ensure:
    
    - Completeness
        
    - Accuracy
        
    - Integrity
        
- Unexpected or suspicious information transfers shall be reported
    

---

## Logging & Monitoring

- Information transfer activities may be logged where appropriate
    
- Logs shall support monitoring, investigation, and audit requirements
    
- Anomalies or failures in transfer controls shall be investigated
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Information Owners

- Approve information transfers
    
- Define transfer and protection requirements
    

### Users

- Use approved transfer methods
    
- Protect information during transfer
    
- Report incidents or suspected data leakage
    

### IT / Operations

- Implement secure transfer mechanisms
    
- Monitor and maintain transfer systems
    

### Information Security Function

- Define information transfer standards
    
- Monitor compliance
    
- Support incident response and improvement
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to comply with information transfer controls may result in:

- Increased risk of information disclosure
    
- Disciplinary action
    
- Contractual or legal consequences
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued effectiveness of transfer mechanisms
    
- Alignment with information classification and risk
    
- Compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements
