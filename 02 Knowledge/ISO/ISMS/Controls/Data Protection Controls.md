# Data Protection Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that information and data are protected against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction throughout their lifecycle, in accordance with business, legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.

Data protection controls support the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information and help prevent data breaches and misuse.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All data processed, stored, or transmitted by the organization
    
- Structured and unstructured data
    
- Personal, confidential, and business- critical information
    
- Data handled by employees, contractors, and third parties
    
- On- premises, cloud, and third- party environments
    

---

## Control References

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Annex A 5.34** - Privacy and protection of personally identifiable information
    
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 - Annex A 8 (multiple controls)** - Secure data handling, storage, transmission
    

(Standard: **ISO/IEC 27001**)

---

## Policy Statement

Data shall be protected throughout its lifecycle using appropriate organizational and technical controls based on its classification, sensitivity, and associated risks.

Data protection requirements shall be integrated into business processes, systems, and services from design through disposal.

---

## Data Protection Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Data is processed lawfully, fairly, and for defined purposes
    
- Data access is restricted based on need- to- know and least privilege
    
- Data accuracy and integrity are maintained
    
- Data is protected against unauthorized disclosure or loss
    
- Data protection measures are proportionate to risk
    

---

## Data Classification & Handling

- Data shall be classified according to defined classification levels
    
- Classification shall determine:
    
    - Storage requirements
        
    - Access controls
        
    - Encryption requirements
        
    - Retention and disposal rules
        
- Data handling procedures shall align with classification
    

---

## Data Access & Authorization

- Access to data shall be formally authorized
    
- Role- based access controls shall be used where feasible
    
- Privileged access to sensitive data shall be limited and monitored
    
- Access rights shall be reviewed periodically
    

---

## Data Storage & Retention

- Data shall be stored in approved locations and systems
    
- Storage mechanisms shall protect against:
    
    - Unauthorized access
        
    - Accidental loss
        
    - Corruption
        
- Data retention periods shall be defined and documented
    
- Data shall not be retained longer than necessary
    

---

## Data Transmission & Sharing

- Data transmitted over networks shall be protected against interception or alteration
    
- Secure communication mechanisms shall be used where required
    
- Data sharing with third parties shall be:
    
    - Authorized
        
    - Documented
        
    - Limited to agreed purposes
        

---

## Data Minimization & Purpose Limitation

- Only data necessary for business purposes shall be collected and processed
    
- Data shall not be reused for incompatible purposes without authorization
    
- Redundant or unnecessary data shall be removed where feasible
    

---

## Data Integrity & Accuracy

- Measures shall be implemented to ensure data accuracy and integrity
    
- Unauthorized modification of data shall be prevented or detected
    
- Integrity controls shall be proportionate to data criticality
    

---

## Data Disposal & Destruction

- Data shall be securely deleted or destroyed when no longer required
    
- Disposal methods shall prevent reconstruction or recovery
    
- Disposal activities shall align with classification and retention requirements
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Data Owners

- Define data protection requirements
    
- Ensure appropriate classification and handling
    
- Approve access and sharing
    

### Users

- Handle data in accordance with policies and procedures
    
- Protect data against unauthorized disclosure
    
- Report suspected data breaches immediately
    

### IT / Operations

- Implement technical data protection controls
    
- Ensure secure storage, backup, and transmission
    

### Information Security Function

- Define data protection requirements
    
- Monitor compliance
    
- Support incident response and improvement activities
    

---

## Third- Party & Cloud Considerations

- Data protection requirements shall be addressed when engaging third parties
    
- Responsibilities for data protection shall be clearly defined
    
- Third- party data protection measures shall be assessed periodically
    

---

## Monitoring & Compliance

- Data protection controls shall be monitored for effectiveness
    
- Data protection incidents shall be handled through incident management processes
    
- Non- compliance shall be addressed through corrective actions
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Alignment with business needs and risks
    
- Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements
    
- Continued effectiveness of protection measures
