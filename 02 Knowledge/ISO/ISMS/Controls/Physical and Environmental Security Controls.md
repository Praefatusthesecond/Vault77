# Physical and Environmental Security Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to prevent unauthorized physical access, damage, interference, or disruption to information, information systems, and supporting facilities.

Physical and environmental security controls protect people, assets, and infrastructure against physical threats and environmental hazards.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All facilities within the scope of the ISMS
    
- Offices, data centers, server rooms, and secure areas
    
- Physical information assets and IT equipment
    
- Environmental conditions affecting information processing facilities
    
- Employees, contractors, visitors, and third parties

## Policy Statement

Physical access to facilities, equipment, and information assets shall be restricted and controlled to prevent unauthorized access, damage, or interference.

Environmental risks to information processing facilities shall be identified and mitigated to maintain availability and safety.

---

## Physical Security Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Physical access is restricted based on authorization and business need
    
- Secure areas are clearly defined and protected
    
- Physical security measures are proportionate to risk and asset value
    
- Environmental risks are identified and mitigated
    

---

## Physical Security Perimeters

- Physical security perimeters shall be defined and maintained
    
- Perimeters may include:
    
    - Walls, doors, locks, fences
        
    - Controlled entry points
        
- Perimeters shall protect areas containing sensitive information or systems
    

---

## Physical Entry Controls

- Access to facilities and secure areas shall be controlled
    
- Entry controls may include:
    
    - Badges or access cards
        
    - Keys or biometric controls
        
    - Visitor registration systems
        
- Access rights shall be reviewed periodically
    

---

## Securing Offices, Rooms, and Facilities

- Offices and rooms containing sensitive assets shall be secured
    
- Doors and access points shall be locked when unattended
    
- Sensitive areas shall not be accessible to unauthorized persons
    

---

## Physical Security Monitoring

- Physical security monitoring shall be implemented where appropriate
    
- Monitoring may include:
    
    - CCTV systems
        
    - Intrusion detection or alarms
        
- Monitoring activities shall comply with legal and privacy requirements
    

---

## Environmental Protection

- Facilities shall be protected against environmental threats such as:
    
    - Fire
        
    - Flooding
        
    - Power failure
        
    - Temperature or humidity extremes
        
- Environmental controls may include:
    
    - Fire detection and suppression systems
        
    - Climate control
        
    - Uninterruptible power supplies (UPS)
        

---

## Equipment Protection

- Equipment shall be protected against physical damage or theft
    
- Measures may include:
    
    - Secure mounting or locking
        
    - Protection against unauthorized removal
        
- Equipment shall be maintained in accordance with manufacturer recommendations
    

---

## Working in Secure Areas

- Secure areas shall be accessible only to authorized personnel
    
- Activities in secure areas shall be monitored where appropriate
    
- Sensitive work shall be performed in a controlled manner
    

---

## Visitor Management

- Visitors shall be identified and recorded
    
- Visitors shall be escorted where required
    
- Visitor access shall be limited to authorized areas and durations
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Management

- Approve physical and environmental security measures
    
- Ensure adequate resources for facility protection
    

### Facility / IT Management

- Implement and maintain physical and environmental controls
    
- Monitor facility conditions and security systems
    

### Information Security Function

- Define physical security requirements
    
- Assess risks and compliance
    
- Support incident response and improvement
    

### All Personnel

- Comply with physical security procedures
    
- Challenge or report suspicious behavior
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to comply with physical and environmental security controls may result in:

- Increased risk of physical or information loss
    
- Disciplinary action
    
- Contractual or legal consequences
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued effectiveness of physical and environmental protections
    
- Alignment with risk assessments and facility changes
    
- Compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements
