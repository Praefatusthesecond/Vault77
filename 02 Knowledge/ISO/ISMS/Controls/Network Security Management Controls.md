# Network Security Management Controls

## Purpose

The purpose of these controls is to ensure that networks and network services are protected against unauthorized access, misuse, disruption, and compromise.

Effective network security management supports the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information by safeguarding data flows, networked systems, and communication paths.

---

## Scope

These controls apply to:

- All organizational networks and network services
    
- Wired, wireless, and virtual networks
    
- On- premises, cloud, and hybrid network environments
    
- Network devices, services, and communication links
    
- Internal, external, and third- party network connections

## Policy Statement

Networks and network services shall be managed and protected to prevent unauthorized access, ensure secure communication, and maintain service availability.

Network security controls shall be proportionate to risk, system criticality, and information classification.

---

## Network Security Principles

The organization shall ensure that:

- Network access is restricted to authorized users, devices, and services
    
- Network traffic is protected against interception and manipulation
    
- Network architectures support segregation and least privilege
    
- Network security controls are documented, monitored, and reviewed
    

---

## Network Architecture & Design

- Network architectures shall be designed with security in mind
    
- Security considerations shall include:
    
    - Segmentation and isolation
        
    - Trust boundaries
        
    - Redundancy and resilience
        
- Network diagrams and documentation shall be maintained
    

---

## Network Access Control

- Access to networks shall be controlled and authorized
    
- Authentication and authorization mechanisms shall be applied where appropriate
    
- Network access rights shall be reviewed periodically
    
- Unauthorized devices or connections shall be prevented or detected
    

---

## Segregation of Networks

- Networks shall be logically or physically segregated based on:
    
    - System criticality
        
    - Information classification
        
    - Risk level
        
- Segregation may include:
    
    - VLANs or subnets
        
    - Firewalls or access control lists
        
    - Virtual network separation
        
- Management, user, and system networks shall be separated where feasible
    

---

## Security of Network Services

- Network services shall be provided securely and reliably
    
- Security controls may include:
    
    - Firewalls and gateways
        
    - Intrusion detection or prevention systems
        
    - Secure routing and switching configurations
        
- Security requirements shall be considered when using third- party network services
    

---

## Protection of Data in Transit

- Network communications shall be protected against interception and alteration
    
- Encryption shall be used where required by classification or risk assessment
    
- Secure communication protocols shall be enforced
    

---

## Wireless Network Security

- Wireless networks shall be secured using approved authentication and encryption mechanisms
    
- Guest or public wireless networks shall be segregated from internal networks
    
- Wireless access shall be monitored for unauthorized use
    

---

## Remote Access & External Connections

- Remote network access shall be authorized and controlled
    
- Secure remote access solutions shall be used
    
- External connections shall be documented and monitored
    

---

## Network Monitoring & Logging

- Network activity shall be logged and monitored where appropriate
    
- Monitoring shall support detection of:
    
    - Unauthorized access
        
    - Malicious activity
        
    - Network failures
        
- Network security events shall feed into incident management processes
    

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Network & System Owners

- Define network security requirements
    
- Approve network access and changes
    

### IT / Operations

- Implement and maintain network security controls
    
- Monitor network performance and security
    

### Information Security Function

- Define network security standards
    
- Review network risks and compliance
    
- Support incident response and improvement
    

---

## Compliance & Enforcement

Failure to comply with network security management controls may result in:

- Increased exposure to cyber threats
    
- Service disruption
    
- Disciplinary, contractual, or legal consequences
    

---

## Review & Maintenance

These controls shall be reviewed periodically to ensure:

- Continued effectiveness of network security measures
    
- Alignment with evolving threats and technologies
    
- Compliance with business, legal, and regulatory requirements
