# ISO/IEC 27001 – Terminology

ISO 27001 relies on precise terminology. Misunderstanding definitions is a common source of audit findings.

This note documents the most important terms **as used within our ISMS**.

## Key Terms

### Asset
Anything that has value to the organization.
Examples: information, systems, people, reputation.

### Threat
A potential cause of an unwanted incident.
Examples: malware, human error, fire, unauthorized access.

### Vulnerability
A weakness that can be exploited by a threat.
Examples: unpatched systems, lack of training, weak procedures.

### Risk
The combination of the likelihood of an event and its impact.

Risk = Threat × Vulnerability × Impact


### Risk Owner
Person responsible for managing a specific risk.

### Risk Treatment
The process of modifying risk.
Options:
- Mitigate
- Avoid
- Transfer
- Accept

### Control
A measure that modifies risk.
Controls can be:
- Preventive
- Detective
- Corrective

Controls are documented under [[Controls Index]].

### Statement of Applicability (SoA)
A document that:
- Lists selected Annex A controls
- Justifies inclusions and exclusions
- Shows implementation status

See [[Statement of Applicability]].

### Residual Risk
Risk remaining after controls have been applied.

### ISMS
Information Security Management System — the overall governance framework.

Clear terminology ensures consistent interpretation across:
- Risk assessments
- Policies
- Audits
- Management reviews
