## 1. Purpose

The Internal Audit ensures the ISMS:

- Conforms to ISO 27001:2022 requirements
    
- Conforms to the organization’s own policies
    
- Is effectively implemented
    
- Is maintained and improved
    

It is required under:

**ISO 27001:2022 Clause 9.2 — Internal Audit**

This is not optional.

---

## 2. What ISO Requires (Clause 9.2 Summary)

The organization must:

- Plan, establish, implement and maintain an audit program
    
- Define audit criteria and scope
    
- Select auditors ensuring objectivity and impartiality
    
- Report results
    
- Retain documented information
    
- Ensure corrective actions are taken
    

Auditors look for process, not perfection.

---

## 3. Internal Audit vs Certification Audit

|Internal Audit|Certification Audit|
|---|---|
|Conducted by organization|Conducted by external body|
|Preventive|Verification|
|Continuous improvement focus|Compliance focus|
|Identifies weaknesses early|Identifies nonconformities formally|

Internal audit is your safety net before the real audit.

---

## 4. Audit Program

An audit program defines:

- Frequency (usually annual minimum)
    
- Scope coverage (entire ISMS over cycle)
    
- Methods (interviews, document review, sampling)
    
- Responsibilities
    
- Reporting structure
    

Example cycle:

- Q1: Governance & Risk
    
- Q2: Technical Controls
    
- Q3: Operations & Incident Handling
    
- Q4: Review & Follow-up
    

Small organizations often audit everything annually.

---

## 5. Audit Scope

Audit scope must align with:

- ISMS Scope
    
- Risk Register
    
- Statement of Applicability
    
- Policies and procedures
    

If something is in scope of the ISMS, it must be auditable.

---

## 6. Audit Criteria

Audit criteria include:

- ISO 27001:2022 clauses
    
- Annex A controls
    
- Internal policies
    
- Legal requirements
    
- Contractual obligations
    

You are auditing against defined requirements.

Not against “best effort.”

---

## 7. Auditor Independence

ISO requires objectivity.

An auditor must not audit their own work.

Examples:

- DevOps engineer cannot audit their own pipeline
    
- CISO should not audit policies they authored
    

In small organizations, this is often addressed via:

- Cross-department audits
    
- External consultant performing internal audit
    

Independence is tested in certification audits.

---

## 8. Audit Process

### 1. Planning

- Define scope
    
- Define criteria
    
- Prepare checklist
    
- Schedule interviews
    

### 2. Execution

- Interview personnel
    
- Review documents
    
- Sample evidence
    
- Validate control implementation
    

### 3. Reporting

- Findings
    
- Nonconformities
    
- Observations
    
- Improvement opportunities
    

### 4. Follow-Up

- Corrective actions assigned
    
- Root cause analysis
    
- Verification of closure
    

---

## 9. Types of Findings

### Major Nonconformity

- Significant failure
    
- Systemic breakdown
    
- Missing required process
    

Example:  
No risk assessment performed.

---

### Minor Nonconformity

- Isolated issue
    
- Process exists but not fully followed
    

Example:  
One policy missing review date.

---

### Observation

- Improvement suggestion
    
- Not a formal nonconformity
    

---

## 10. Required Evidence

Auditor will expect:

- Documented audit program
    
- Audit plan
    
- Audit reports
    
- Evidence of auditor competence
    
- Corrective action records
    
- Follow-up verification
    

If you cannot show closure tracking, that’s a finding.

---

## 11. Internal Audit Checklist (Core Areas)

Typical ISO 27001 internal audit covers:

### Clause 4

- Context of organization
    
- Interested parties
    
- ISMS scope
    

### Clause 5

- Leadership commitment
    
- Information security policy
    

### Clause 6

- Risk assessment
    
- Risk treatment plan
    

### Clause 7

- Competence
    
- Awareness
    
- Document control
    

### Clause 8

- Operational control
    
- Risk treatment implementation
    

### Clause 9

- Monitoring
    
- Measurement
    
- Management review
    

### Clause 10

- Nonconformity and corrective action
    

Plus Annex A controls.

---

## 12. Relationship to Management Review

Internal audit results feed into:

- Management Review (Clause 9.3)
    
- Continuous improvement
    
- Resource allocation
    
- Strategic risk decisions
    

No audit findings → suspicious.

Healthy ISMS always finds improvement areas.

---

## 13. DevSecOps-Oriented Internal Audit Focus

For modern environments, audit should verify:

- CI/CD pipeline security
    
- Access control enforcement
    
- Logging and monitoring coverage
    
- Backup restoration testing
    
- Infrastructure as Code controls
    
- Container hardening
    
- Cloud security posture
    
- MFA enforcement
    

Auditors increasingly expect cloud and DevOps maturity.

---

## 14. Common Audit Failures

- Audit program exists but never executed
    
- No documented evidence retained
    
- Auditor auditing own department
    
- Findings not tracked to closure
    
- Risk register not reviewed during audit
    
- Scope mismatch
    

---

## 15. Minimal Audit Program Template (Structure)

Your internal audit documentation should include:

- Audit Program (annual overview)
    
- Audit Plan (per audit)
    
- Audit Checklist
    
- Audit Report
    
- Corrective Action Log
    
- Closure Verification Record
    

Version-controlled.

Management approved.

---

## 16. Interview-Level Explanation

If asked:

“What is the purpose of an ISO 27001 internal audit?”

Answer:

An internal audit evaluates whether the ISMS conforms to ISO 27001 requirements and the organization’s own policies, verifies effective implementation, and identifies improvement opportunities before certification audits.

Concise. Executive-level.

---

## 17. Governance Maturity Notes

Mature organizations:

- Conduct risk-based audits
    
- Use sampling techniques
    
- Maintain auditor training records
    
- Track metrics on recurring findings
    
- Integrate audit with continuous improvement cycle
    

Internal audit is not paperwork.

It is operational discipline.
