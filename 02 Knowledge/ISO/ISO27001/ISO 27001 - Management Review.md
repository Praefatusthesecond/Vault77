## 1. Purpose

Management Review ensures:

- The ISMS remains suitable
    
- The ISMS remains adequate
    
- The ISMS remains effective
    
- Strategic alignment with business objectives
    

It is required under:

**ISO 27001:2022 Clause 9.3 — Management Review**

This is a **top management responsibility**.

Not IT.  
Not DevOps.  
Not security alone.

Top management.

---

## 2. Why It Matters

Management Review demonstrates:

- Leadership commitment (Clause 5)
    
- Strategic oversight
    
- Risk ownership at executive level
    
- Continuous improvement
    
- Resource allocation decisions
    

Without it, ISO becomes a paper exercise.

Auditors check this carefully.

---

## 3. Frequency

ISO does not mandate frequency.

Best practice:

- At least annually
    
- More frequently in high-risk environments
    
- After major incidents
    
- After significant organizational changes
    

Consistency matters more than frequency.

---

## 4. Required Inputs (Clause 9.3.2)

ISO defines required inputs.

Management review must consider:

- Status of previous actions
    
- Changes in external/internal issues
    
- Feedback from interested parties
    
- Information security performance
    
- Fulfilment of security objectives
    
- Audit results
    
- Nonconformities and corrective actions
    
- Monitoring and measurement results
    
- Risk assessment and treatment status
    
- Opportunities for improvement
    

If these are not discussed → nonconformity.

---

## 5. Required Outputs (Clause 9.3.3)

Management Review must produce decisions regarding:

- Opportunities for improvement
    
- Changes to ISMS
    
- Resource needs
    

There must be documented evidence.

Not “we talked about it.”

Documented.

---

## 6. Typical Management Review Agenda

A structured review might include:

1. Opening & objectives
    
2. ISMS performance summary
    
3. Risk landscape update
    
4. Incident overview
    
5. Audit findings summary
    
6. KPI review
    
7. Resource & budget discussion
    
8. Improvement proposals
    
9. Decisions and action items
    

Executive-focused. Not technical deep dive.

---

## 7. Evidence Required for Audit

Auditor will expect:

- Management Review meeting minutes
    
- Attendee list (including top management)
    
- Agenda
    
- Inputs reviewed
    
- Decisions taken
    
- Action items with owners
    
- Follow-up tracking
    

If management is absent → red flag.

---

## 8. Relationship to Other ISO Elements

Management Review ties everything together:

|ISMS Element|Reviewed During Management Review|
|---|---|
|Risk Register|Yes|
|Internal Audit|Yes|
|Incident Records|Yes|
|KPIs|Yes|
|Objectives|Yes|
|Corrective Actions|Yes|
|Resource Planning|Yes|

It is the strategic consolidation point.

---

## 9. Common Audit Findings

- No documented evidence
    
- Management not actively involved
    
- Inputs incomplete
    
- Outputs missing clear decisions
    
- Actions not tracked to closure
    
- Same findings recurring yearly
    

Management Review must show progress.

---

## 10. DevSecOps-Oriented Management Review Focus

In modern environments, review should include:

- CI/CD security posture
    
- Cloud risk exposure
    
- Container vulnerability trends
    
- Privileged access management metrics
    
- Backup restoration testing results
    
- Incident response maturity
    
- Supply chain risks
    
- Monitoring coverage gaps
    

Executives should understand:

- What could stop the business?
    
- What is our exposure?
    
- Are we improving?
    

---

## 11. Example Management Review Output

Example documented decision:

> Based on increased cloud expansion, management approves additional budget for centralized logging and vulnerability management tooling.

That is a proper output.

Strategic.  
Resource-driven.  
Actionable.

---

## 12. KPIs Commonly Reviewed

Examples:

- Number of open high risks
    
- Mean time to detect (MTTD)
    
- Mean time to respond (MTTR)
    
- % of systems with MFA enabled
    
- % of critical vulnerabilities patched within SLA
    
- Backup restore success rate
    
- Internal audit finding closure rate
    

Without metrics, review becomes subjective.

---

## 13. Governance Maturity Levels

Basic:

- Annual meeting
    
- Formal sign-off
    

Intermediate:

- KPI tracking
    
- Resource decisions
    
- Trend analysis
    

Advanced:

- Risk appetite discussions
    
- Strategic investment alignment
    
- Cyber resilience planning
    
- Scenario testing

Your vault should ideally include:

- Management Review Policy
    
- Management Review Agenda Template
    
- Management Review Minutes Template
    
- Action Item Log
    
- KPI Dashboard Reference
    

Version-controlled.

Approved by top management.

---

## 15. Interview-Level Explanation

If asked:

“What is the purpose of ISO 27001 Management Review?”

Answer:

Management Review ensures top management evaluates the performance, risks, and effectiveness of the ISMS, makes strategic decisions, and allocates resources to ensure continuous improvement and alignment with business objectives.

Clear. Executive-level.

---

## 16. Key Principle

Internal Audit asks:

“Are we compliant?”

Management Review asks:

“Are we secure enough for where the business is going?”

That distinction is maturity.
