# Continuous Improvement

ISO 27001 requires continual improvement of the ISMS.

Security is not static — threats, technology and business contexts change continuously.

## PDCA Cycle

ISO 27001 is built on the **Plan–Do–Check–Act (PDCA)** model.

### Plan
- Define scope
- Perform risk assessment
- Define controls
- Set objectives

References:
- [[ISMS Scope]]
- [[Risk Register]]
- [[Statement of Applicability]]

### Do
- Implement controls
- Train personnel
- Operate processes

References:
- [[Controls]]
- [[Information Security Awareness and Training Controls]]

### Check
- Monitor controls
- Review incidents
- Perform audits
- Measure performance

References:
- [[Logging and Monitoring Controls]]
- [[Incident Management]]
- [[Internal Audit]]

### Act
- Address nonconformities
- Improve controls
- Update risk assessments

References:
- [[Corrective Actions]]
- [[Management Review]]

## Evidence of Improvement

Auditors typically expect:
- Updated risk assessments
- Revised policies
- Control enhancements
- Lessons learned from incidents

## Our Improvement Mechanisms
- Management reviews
- Incident post-mortems
- Audit findings
- Risk reassessments

Continuous improvement demonstrates **governance maturity**, not just compliance.
