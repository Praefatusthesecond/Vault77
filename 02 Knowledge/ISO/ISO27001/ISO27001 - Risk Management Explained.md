# Risk Management Explained

ISO 27001 requires organizations to identify, assess and treat information security risks.

## Risk Flow
1. Identify assets
2. Identify threats and vulnerabilities
3. Assess likelihood and impact
4. Determine risk level
5. Select treatment options
6. Apply controls
7. Accept residual risk

## Risk Treatment Options
- Mitigate
- Avoid
- Transfer
- Accept

## Implementation
- Risks are recorded in [[ISO 27001 - Risk Register]]
- Controls selected are justified in [[Statement of Applicability]]
