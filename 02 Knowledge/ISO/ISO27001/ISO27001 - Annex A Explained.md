# Annex A Explained

Annex A is a reference set of information security controls.

## Key Points
- Controls are **not mandatory by default**
- Selection is based on risk assessment
- Exclusions must be justified

## Control Domains
Annex A controls are grouped into thematic areas such as:
- Organizational controls
- People controls
- Physical controls
- Technological controls
