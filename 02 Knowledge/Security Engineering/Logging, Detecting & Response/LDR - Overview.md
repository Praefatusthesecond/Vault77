# Logging, Detection & Response – Overview

Logging, Detection & Response (LDR) is the defensive backbone of modern infrastructure.

Security controls prevent.
Detection proves.
Response limits damage.

Without logging, you are blind.
Without detection, you are slow.
Without response, you are compromised.

---

## Objectives

- Gain visibility into systems
- Detect malicious behavior
- Respond quickly
- Preserve forensic evidence
- Improve continuously

---

## Blue Team in DevSecOps

DevSecOps builds secure systems.
Blue Team validates and defends them.

Both must work together.
