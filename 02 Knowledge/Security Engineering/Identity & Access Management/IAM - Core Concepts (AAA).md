# IAM – Core Concepts (AAA)

IAM is built on AAA:

## Authentication
Who are you?

## Authorization
What can you do?

## Accounting (Auditing)
What did you do?

---

## Identity Types

- Human users
- Service accounts
- Applications
- CI/CD pipelines
- Managed identities
- Federated identities

---

## Principle of Least Privilege

Grant:
Only what is needed.
Only for as long as needed.
Only from where needed.

---

## Defense Strategy

- Strong authentication
- Fine-grained authorization
- Continuous monitoring
