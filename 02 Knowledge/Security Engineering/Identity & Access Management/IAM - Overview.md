# Identity & Access Management – Overview

IAM controls:

- Who can access what
- Under which conditions
- With what level of privilege
- For how long

Most cloud breaches are IAM failures.

---

## IAM Goals

- Enforce least privilege
- Prevent privilege escalation
- Provide auditability
- Enable secure automation
- Support Zero Trust

---

## IAM Scope in DevSecOps

- Developer access
- CI/CD permissions
- Cloud control plane access
- Application authentication
- Machine-to-machine identity
- Secret retrieval
