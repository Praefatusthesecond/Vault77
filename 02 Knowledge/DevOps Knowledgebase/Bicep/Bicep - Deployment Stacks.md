# Bicep - Deployment Stacks

Deployment Stacks help manage a **set of resources as a unit**, including lifecycle and (optionally) cleanup behavior.

## When stacks shine
- Platform teams managing landing zones
- Repeatable environment deployments
- Guarding against orphaned resources

## Things to watch
- Deletion/cleanup settings are powerful—treat like production explosives: label clearly, use approvals.
