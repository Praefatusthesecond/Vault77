# Zabbix

Zabbix is an enterprise-grade monitoring platform used to monitor infrastructure, services, applications, and networks in real time.

It supports:
- Agent-based and agentless monitoring
- Metrics, logs, and availability checks
- Alerting, escalation, and remediation
- Automation and discovery at scale
