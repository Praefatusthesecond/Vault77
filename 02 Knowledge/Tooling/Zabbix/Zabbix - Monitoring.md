# Monitoring

Monitoring is the practice of collecting metrics and signals from systems to detect failures, degradation, or abnormal behavior.

## What monitoring answers
- Is the system **up**?
- Is it **healthy**?
- Is it **getting worse**?

## Common signals
- Availability (uptime)
- Performance (latency, throughput)
- Resource usage (CPU, memory, disk)
- Errors and failures

## Tooling
- [[Zabbix - Overview]]
- Prometheus
- Nagios
- Cloud-native monitoring

## Monitoring vs Observability
Monitoring tells you **that** something is wrong.  
Observability helps you understand **why**.
