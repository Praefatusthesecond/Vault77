# Contact points

## What they are
Definitions for notification targets: email, Slack, Teams, PagerDuty, Opsgenie, webhooks.

## Operational tips
- Prefer integration tokens scoped to the minimum permissions.
- Keep “who owns this channel” documented.
- Test contact points regularly (quarterly at minimum).
